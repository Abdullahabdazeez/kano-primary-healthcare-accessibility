## First prepared release

This release presents a network-based assessment of primary healthcare accessibility and spatial equity across Kano State.

### Included

- Validated PHC distribution and population-demand data
- Road-network travel-time analysis
- Population-weighted 5-, 10- and 15-minute accessibility
- 15-minute Two-Step Floating Catchment Area modelling
- Multi-dimensional underserved-ward classification
- Healthcare planning-priority analysis
- Strategic +10 PHC expansion scenario
- Six final maps and seven analytical charts
- Selected GIS layers and result tables
- Reproduction and repository-validation scripts
- Methodology, limitations and interpretation documentation

### Principal findings

- 97.78% of the population was within 10 minutes of a PHC.
- Population-weighted nearest-PHC travel time was 2.45 minutes.
- Population-weighted 2SFCA accessibility was 0.8438 PHCs per 10,000 people.
- 84 critical or compound underserved wards contained 2,759,236 people.
- 97 wards were classified as very-high planning priorities.
- The preferred +10 PHC scenario improved modelled 2SFCA access for 556,701 people.
