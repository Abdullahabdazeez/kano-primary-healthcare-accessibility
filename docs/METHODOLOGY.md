# Methodology

The workflow integrated validated PHC locations, a one-kilometre population-demand grid, administrative boundaries and an OpenStreetMap drive network.

Travel time was estimated through multi-source shortest-path analysis after assigning speed assumptions by road class. Accessibility was summarised with population-weighted 5-, 10- and 15-minute thresholds.

A 15-minute Two-Step Floating Catchment Area model first calculated facility-to-population ratios within each PHC catchment and then summed accessible facility ratios for every demand location. Ward and LGA summaries were derived from the demand-level results.

Underserved wards were classified from the joint distribution of travel-time access, 2SFCA supply and population exposure. A composite planning-priority score then identified wards with the strongest combined need. Candidate PHC scenarios evaluated five, ten and twenty additional facilities, with the ten-site scenario retained as the preferred intervention balance.
