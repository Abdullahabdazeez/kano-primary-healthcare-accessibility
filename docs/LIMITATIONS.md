# Limitations

- Road speeds are model assumptions based on road class rather than observed traffic conditions.
- OpenStreetMap completeness varies spatially and temporally.
- The analysis measures geographic access and modelled supply pressure, not quality of care, staffing, medicines, opening hours or affordability.
- 2SFCA results depend on the selected 15-minute catchment and demand-allocation assumptions.
- Priority and underserved categories are relative analytical classes, not official service standards.
- Candidate PHC locations require parcel-level feasibility, land ownership, environmental, financial and community assessment.
